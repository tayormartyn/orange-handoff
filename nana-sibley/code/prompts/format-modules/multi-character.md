# Layer 3 — Format Module: Multi-Character

> For hand-offs and the twins' reveal. More than one voice in one script.

```text
FORMAT: multi_character
PURPOSE: a single script spoken by two (or three) characters — a warm
hand-off of the emotional baton (comfort → feel → carry).
TARGET LENGTH: 35–60 seconds spoken (richer, not longer).
STRUCTURE RULES:
  - Every line MUST carry a "speaker" tag so audio routes to the correct
    locked voice.
  - NAN INITIATES and blesses. The twins inherit her trust by building on
    her ("Nan gave you the comfort — now what will you do with it?").
  - The twins never overrule or eclipse Nan; they complete her.
  - One clean pass of the baton, not a debate. Keep hand-offs short/warm.
  - Each character stays 100% in their own lexicon and ritual.
USE CASES: the twins' introduction/reveal; a Nan→Jesse integration piece;
a Nan→Maeve comfort-to-feeling piece.
ENDING: the character who closes uses THEIR closing ritual + the dream
invitation.
```

The keeper of the garden

"There's no such thing as a silly dream by this fire, my love."

Nana Sybil is a warm place out of time — a stone cottage, a wild garden full of poppies and foxgloves, and a kettle that's always on. Each day she reads a dream, tells an old story, and offers a moment of quiet before you sleep.

She carries a real family's tradition of dream-lore — handed down through Romani Traveller roots and an Irish gift for reading dreams — and she's brought to life with AI, with a real person behind every reading. Her words are for comfort and gentle reflection, always offered with care.

So whatever kind of day you've had… wander in. The gate's open, and there's a chair waiting.

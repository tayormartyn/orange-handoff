# Episode 1 — "Come and sit with me a minute" (the flagship)

*Sybil's Hearth · the definitive first video · made to travel · v1.0*

**The strategy:** don't teach, don't read a symbol — just *see* the viewer.
The most shareable thing a warm old woman can do to a tired stranger at night
is notice them and tell them they've done well. That's the whole video. It
disarms in 2 seconds, lands an emotional gut-punch in the middle, and ends on
the dream invitation that starts the loop.

Target: **~45 seconds · vertical 9:16 · Nan solo.**

---

## The script (breath-scored — the voice worker adds gravity breaths)

> **[0–2s · HOOK — she looks up from the fire, straight down the lens]**
> "Oh… there you are. Come here to me a minute, love."
>
> **[the invite — warm, low]**
> "Sit yourself down. Go on. You don't have to do a thing here — you don't even have to talk. Just sit with me a while, by the fire."
>
> **[validation — she sees them]**
> "You've had a day of it, haven't you. I can see it in your face, my darling. One of those weeks where you've been carrying it all… and nobody's noticed."
>
> **[the turn — a bit of hard-won wisdom]**
> "Can I tell you something my own nan told me? She said — you don't have to carry the whole week tonight, pet. Just tonight. Just the next little bit. That's all anyone can do."
>
> **[THE LANDING — the gravity point, the shareable line]**
> "And you've been so strong. Longer than anyone knows.
>
> …Nobody's said it, so let me. You've done ever so well, love. I'm proud of you. I am."
>
> **[close + the loop]**
> "Now — go and get yourself something warm, and rest. And before you go tonight… leave me a dream, down below. Any dream at all. Every week I take one, and I read it, right here by the fire.
>
> The kettle's on, love. …I'll be here."

*(A soft, closed-mouth smile. Hold two beats. Loop the last frame to the first.)*

# Nana Sybil — Publishing Pack (copy-paste for every video)

> Reusable title / description / caption / pinned-comment templates for YouTube
> and TikTok. Swap the [BRACKETS] for the dream or topic. Keep the AI-disclosure
> ON every time — that's Rule Zero.

---

## YOUTUBE SHORTS

**TITLE** (make it searchable — lead with the dream, add a hook + a candle):
`What it means when you dream of [TOPIC] 🕯️`
or `Dreaming of [TOPIC]? Here's what the old stories really say 🕯️`

**DESCRIPTION** (paste, edit the one warm line):
```
Come and sit by the fire a minute, love. 🕯️

[One warm line about this dream — e.g. "If your teeth crumbled in a dream last night, don't fret — here's what the old folk believed."]

Drop your dream in the comments below — I read them here, by the fire. 🌙

Nana Sybil is an AI-crafted character, made with love and a real family's tradition of dream-lore. Her readings are for comfort and reflection, and are not a substitute for professional support.

#dreammeaning #dreams #dreaminterpretation #comfort #grandma #bedtime #shorts
```

**PINNED COMMENT:**
```
Drop your dream in the comments, my darling — I read them here by the fire. 🌙 And if you needed to hear it today: you've done ever so well.
```

**AI disclosure:** during upload, tick "Altered content → Yes". Every time.

## Quick rules
- **Title = the dream + a hook.** People search "dream about X meaning" — put those words in.
- **One specific question now and then** in the pinned comment ("What dream keeps coming back to you?") — it spikes replies.
- **Same clip, all three platforms.** Export clean (no other platform's watermark).
- **AI label ON everywhere.** Non-negotiable.

## Hashtag bank (niche, not generic)

Skip #fyp #foryou #viral — too broad, they don't help. Use 4–6 niche tags that
describe the content. Pick one from each row + the specific dream.

- Dream niche: `#dreammeaning #dreaminterpretation #dreamsymbols #dreamanalysis #dreamtok`
- Cosy / folklore: `#cottagecore #folklore #grandmacore #oldsoul #cozycore`
- The feeling: `#comfortingwords #gentlereminder #youmatter #bekindtoyourself`
- Bedtime / sleep: `#bedtimestory #sleeptok #fallasleep #nighttimecomfort`
- Per-video (swap in the dream): `#teethdream #fallingdream #flyingdream #beingchaseddream #waterdream`

Examples:
- Teeth: `#dreammeaning #dreamtok #teethdream #comfortingwords #cottagecore`
- Dream reading: `#dreaminterpretation #dreamtok #comfortingwords #cottagecore #sleeptok`

Remember: hashtags are a SMALL lever. The video's hook (first 2 seconds), the
captions, and watch-time matter far more. Don't stuff 20 tags — 4–6 is plenty.

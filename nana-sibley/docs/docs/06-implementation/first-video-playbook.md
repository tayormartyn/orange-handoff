# The First Video Playbook — get one Nan short live this week

*Sybil's Hearth · the finish line that actually matters · v1.0*

Stop building machine. Make **one** video, by hand, and put it in the world.
Everything in this repo exists to serve this moment — and until a real person
watches Nan and feels something, we're guessing. This is the honest finish
line for Phase 1.

---

## The call: start with a single-Nan short

**Not** the three-character Rising Tide (three faces is the hardest thing to
make look premium). Start with **"A blessing for a hard day"** — Nan Sybil
playbook, Video 2. Thirty-five seconds, one face, one voice, pure warmth. It's
her share-magnet, and it's the emotional heart. If this lands, the whole idea
is proven.

The script is already written (`docs/01-characters/nan-sybil-playbook.md`,
Video 2). You don't need to draft anything — it's ready.

---

## Step 1 — Approve the script (your gate)
Read it aloud once. Does it give a stranger what your nan gave you? If yes,
it's approved. (It already passed Rule Zero and safety by design.)

## Step 2 — Voice it (ElevenLabs, ~10 min)
On your machine:
```bash
export ELEVEN_API_KEY="..."   ELEVEN_VOICE_NAN="yourNanVoiceId"
python3 build/hearth_voice.py --script blessing.json
```
- Use the **three-take rule** on the two or three key lines — render a couple,
  keep the warmest.
- The gravity-point breaths are added automatically. Listen back: does the
  silence *land*? If a pause feels rushed, add ` … ` in the script and re-render.
- You now have Nan's audio. **This is the file HeyGen will lip-sync to.**

## Step 3 — HeyGen render (the craft step — budget an evening)
1. Create Nan's face as a **Photo Avatar / Avatar IV** from a single locked
   portrait (warm, elderly, kind — your auntie/family likeness, per the Bible;
   never a celebrity). Approve one image and **lock it**.
2. Choose **"upload audio"** (audio-driven), and give it your ElevenLabs file —
   so her mouth matches *your* voice and breaths, not HeyGen's TTS.
3. Settings for premium, not slop:
   - **Vertical 9:16**, HD.
   - **Warm, close portrait** framing — head and shoulders, fireside feel.
   - A fireside/cottage **background** (upload a warm still, or a soft dark
     amber). Never flat white light.
   - Calm, minimal motion. Stillness reads as presence.
4. Render, watch it back, and **re-render until it's not sloppy** — soft eyes,
   a natural closed-mouth warmth, no uncanny darting. This iteration IS the
   work; take the time.

## Step 4 — Assemble & caption (CapCut is easiest, ~30 min)
- Import the HeyGen clip. Add **burned-in captions** in a warm serif or gentle
  handwritten font (never a hard tech sans). Most people watch muted.
- Add a soft **fire-crackle ambience** low under her voice, and a gentle warm
  vignette/grain (it reads cosy and hides AI sheen).
- Make the **last frame loop** back to the first if you can.
- Export vertical, clean, no watermark.

## Step 5 — Upload to YouTube, by hand (~10 min)
Do this manually — it's where you control the things that matter:
- Post as a **Short**. Title with the words people search:
  *"When you've had one of those days 🕯️"* or *"A little comfort before you sleep."*
- Description: warm one-liner + *"Leave your dream below — Nan reads one every week."*
- **Turn ON the altered/AI-content disclosure toggle.** Required, and it's Rule
  Zero. Non-negotiable.
- **Pin a comment** inviting dreams — this seeds the Dream-of-the-Week loop from
  video one.
- A few hashtags: #dreammeaning #comfort #bedtime.
- Cross-post the same clean file to **TikTok** (label it AI there too) and Reels.

## Step 6 — Then watch, and let the audience judge
- Look at **retention** (did they stay?) and **comments** (did the loop start?).
- Don't read the world into one video. Make the next four the same way. Five
  Nan shorts is a real test; one is a coin-toss.

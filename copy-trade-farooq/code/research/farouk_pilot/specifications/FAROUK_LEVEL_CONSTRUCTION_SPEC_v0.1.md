# FAROUK LEVEL-CONSTRUCTION SPECIFICATION — v0.1

**Status: DRAFT / EVIDENCE-GRADED. Descriptive only — not an algorithm, not executable.**
How the levels Farouk trades appear to be constructed, from directly-visible chart labels, the on-screen
custom indicator panel, and spoken descriptions (FP-EDU-001 + FP-CAMPAIGN-001/002/003). No market data
has been used, so **none of these constructions has been independently reproduced from price**. Tags:
**[STRONG]** directly visible + consistent · **[PARTIAL]** visible but under-specified · **[OPEN]** unknown.

> This document does not contain executable detector code and must not be treated as one.

---

## A. Charting environment (context for the levels)
- **[STRONG]** Platform **TradingView**, usually in **Bar-Replay** (retrospective). Feeds observed:
  **Pepperstone, Vantage, FXCM** (they change between/within videos) and crypto on **Bybit**. Instrument
  primarily **XAUUSD** (Gold Spot / U.S. Dollar). Platform timezone seen as **UTC+2** (FP-EDU-001).
- **[STRONG]** A **custom indicator panel** (bottom-right) reports, per timeframe: `TF`, `CHoCH`,
  `Asia break` (HIGH/LOW), `OB retest`, `Current OB`, `Fresh OB`. Values are **timeframe-dependent** and
  auto-generated by the indicator.

## B. Session levels
- **[STRONG]** **Asia High** and **Asia Low** — horizontal lines at the Asian-session range extremes;
  drawn/labelled on every campaign chart and driving the panel `Asia break HIGH/LOW`.
- **[PARTIAL]** **London High** — session-high liquidity referenced verbally (C003) but not always drawn.
- **[OPEN]** The exact **session windows** (start/end clock times, and in which timezone) used to compute
  the Asia/London ranges. The `Asia break` flag flips HIGH/LOW but its trigger rule is not specified.

## C. Order Blocks (OB)
- **[STRONG]** **Order Blocks** are the origin candle/zone of a move ("the level that made that high"),
  drawn on multiple timeframes (5m/15m/1h/4H/weekly). Used as support/resistance on **retest**.
- **[STRONG]** Panel fields **`OB retest`**, **`Current OB`**, **`Fresh OB`** carry OB price values
  (e.g. C001 OB retest 4123.51; C003 OB retest 4009.08 / Current OB 4009.08 / Fresh OB 4379.08).
- **[PARTIAL]** Distinction between **Current OB** vs **Fresh OB** vs an **old/mitigated OB**: Farouk says a
  **mitigated** OB should not be re-entered (C003), and an "old OB / breaker OB" can still act as S/R (EDU).
- **[OPEN]** The precise algorithm the indicator uses to detect, refresh, and invalidate an OB.

## D. Fair Value Gaps (FVG) / imbalance
- **[STRONG]** **FVG** (fair value gap / imbalance) zones are drawn and labelled; a **Daily FVG** is used as
  a higher-timeframe magnet/target (C003) and as a continuation gate ("1h close above the daily bearish
  FVG"). **BPR** (balanced price range) is also referenced (EDU, C001).
- **[PARTIAL]** "Bearish/bullish" and "M15 / H4 / Weekly" FVG labels appear (C002 frames). Direction and TF
  are annotated but the gap-definition rule is not stated.
- **[OPEN]** Exact FVG detection (3-candle imbalance vs other), and mitigation/refresh rules.

## E. Market structure — CHoCH / BOS
- **[STRONG]** **Break of Structure (BOS)** and **Change of Character (CHoCH)** are labelled on-chart and
  the panel reports a `CHoCH` value (or `×` when none). BOS defines trend; CHoCH flags a shift used as *a*
  confirmation (not always required — C003 win had CHoCH ×).
- **[OPEN]** The swing-detection basis (what counts as the structural high/low) behind BOS/CHoCH.

## F. Liquidity
- **[STRONG]** **Resting liquidity** at session extremes is central ("liquidity resting here", "trend
  liquidity", `$$$` marker). Sweeps of Asia High/Low (and London high) are entries/vetoes depending on
  follow-through.
- **[PARTIAL]** "Tap the liquidity then reverse" (C003) as a reversal trigger at the opposing extreme.

## G. Higher-timeframe zones
- **[STRONG]** HTF **supply/demand** zones (red/pink and blue bands) and HTF **OB/FVG** (H4 OB, H4/M15/Weekly
  bearish FVG, Weekly CHoCH — C002 frames) provide trade **location**; entries are preferred **at** these.
- **[OPEN]** How HTF zones are prioritised/scored when several overlap.

## H. Pip / value convention (recorded, not asserted)
- **[STRONG]** A **0.10** XAUUSD price move is strongly consistent with **one channel "pip"** (C003 result
  cards); result value ≈ **price_move × 100 × displayed_size**; `buy 1 / 0.5 / 0.25` consistent with **lot**
  sizes. **[OPEN]** No **currency symbol** is shown on the values — the unit remains UNKNOWN and recorded.

01
Fair Value Gaps
An FVG is a price imbalance — a gap in price action created when
institutions move the market aggressively in one direction. Price
often returns to fill these gaps, giving us high-probability entry zones.
■ BULLISH FVG
Forms when candle 1's high is lower than
candle 3's low — with a big bullish candle 2 in
between. The gap is the area of imbalance
that price will likely return to fill.
TRADE IT: Wait for price to retrace into the FVG.
Enter long with SL below the FVG. Target = next
liquidity level.
■ BEARISH FVG
Forms when candle 1's low is higher than
candle 3's high — with a big bearish candle 2
in between. Price will likely return to fill this
gap before continuing down.
TRADE IT: Wait for price to retrace UP into the
FVG. Enter short with SL above the FVG. Target =
next liquidity level below.
■ FVG RULES
1. Higher timeframe FVGs are more reliable (4H, Daily > 5M, 15M)
2. Best when aligned with overall market structure
3. Once filled, the FVG is invalid — don't trade it again
4. Look for confirmation candle (hammer, engulfing) at the FVG

FVG Continuation Setup
The classic 5-minute bottom reversal play
When price is bottoming out and starts pushing up, watch for an FVG to form on the 5min. If a
candle closes ABOVE the FVG, the setup is confirmed. Price will likely retrace to claim/fill the
FVG, then continue UP toward the next bearish Order Block. This is one of the cleanest
XAUUSD setups during London/NY session.
■ THE PLAY — STEP BY STEP   ● green = FVG  ● aqua = bearish OB
1. Price is in a downtrend and starts bottoming (hammer / morning star / rejection wicks).
2. Price impulses UP, leaving a clean bullish FVG behind on the 5min.
3. KEY MOMENT: wait for a candle to CLOSE above the FVG — body close, not just a
wick.
4. Price pulls back to the FVG to claim/fill it (your entry zone).
5. Bullish reaction at the FVG → enter LONG.
6. Target: the next bearish Order Block above.
■ TRADE EXECUTION  Best on 5min XAUUSD — London open (08:00 UTC) & NY open
(13:30 UTC)
Entry: at the FVG fill with bullish confirmation candle    SL: below the FVG bottom
TP1: midpoint between FVG and OB    TP2: at the bearish OB    R:R: 1:3 or better

02
Balanced Price Range
The BPR is the most powerful FVG setup — formed when a bullish
FVG and a bearish FVG OVERLAP at the same price level. The
overlap creates a high-precision reaction zone where price almost
always responds.
HOW TO SPOT A BPR (3 STEPS)
1. Mark all FVGs on your chart — both bullish and bearish
2. Find an overlap — where a bullish FVG sits at the same price as a bearish
FVG
3. That overlap zone = your BPR. Wait for price to return there.
■ BPR TRADE SETUP
BULLISH BPR: Enter LONG at the BPR. SL below the swing low. TP = next
liquidity above.
BEARISH BPR: Enter SHORT at the BPR. SL above the swing high. TP = next
liquidity below.
BONUS: If the BPR aligns with a candlestick reversal pattern (Hammer,
Shooting Star, etc.) — that's an A+++ setup.

03
Order Blocks & Liquidity
Order Blocks are the last opposing candle before a strong impulsive
move — where institutions placed their orders. Liquidity is where
stops are clustered. Smart money hunts these zones.
■ ORDER BLOCK
The LAST bearish candle before a strong
bullish move (bullish OB) — or the LAST
bullish candle before a strong bearish move
(bearish OB). When price returns to this
zone, it acts as strong support/resistance.
TRADE IT: Enter when price returns to the OB.
SL beyond the OB body. Target = previous
high/low.
■ LIQUIDITY SWEEP
Price spikes below equal lows (or above
equal highs) to grab stops, then reverses
sharply. Smart money does this to fuel the
real move. Look for a wick that breaks the
level then closes back inside.
TRADE IT: After the sweep, wait for a reversal
candle (Hammer/Shooting Star). Enter in the
direction of the reversal.
■ WHERE TO FIND LIQUIDITY
 Below equal lows (sellside liquidity)
 Above equal highs (buyside liquidity)
 Below recent swing lows
 Above recent swing highs
 Asian session high & low
 Previous day's high & low (PDH/PDL)
 Round numbers (4500, 4550, 4600)

The Engulfing Candle
The Engulfing is one of the strongest reversal patterns. It happens when a candle's body
completely covers (engulfs) the previous candle's body — showing a powerful momentum shift.
■ BULLISH ENGULFING
A small bearish candle followed by a
large green candle that fully engulfs the
body of the previous one. Buyers took
complete control.
BEST AT: Support, FVG, or BPR at the
bottom of a downtrend.
■ BEARISH ENGULFING
A small bullish candle followed by a large
red candle that fully engulfs the body of
the previous one. Sellers took complete
control.
BEST AT: Resistance, FVG, or BPR at
the top of an uptrend.
HOW IT LOOKS ON A CHART
■ ENGULFING TRADE RULES
1. The engulfing body must be 2x larger than the previous candle
2. Best when it engulfs at an FVG, BPR, or Order Block
3. Entry: open of next candle      SL: beyond the engulfing wick
4. If engulfing happens after a liquidity sweep = A+++ confluence

05
Asia Session Setup
One of the cleanest setups: the Asia session creates a range, then
London or NY tries to break the Asia high — but FAILS to close
above it. After that failure, if price makes a lower low → bearish trap
confirmed. Time to short.
■ THE SETUP — STEP BY STEP
1. Mark the Asia session high & low (gold dashed = high, red dashed = low) —
the range usually forms during the Asian trading hours.
2. London opens — price often pushes UP toward the Asia high.
3. Watch carefully: does any candle CLOSE above the Asia high? If the wick
pokes through but the body closes back below → FAKEOUT.
4. Price reverses. The first lower low below the Asia low = confirmation. The
trap is sprung.
5. Short on retracement. SL above the failed high. TP = next major liquidity zone
below.
■ WHY THIS WORKS
Asia traders place buy stops above the Asia high — they expect a breakout. Smart
money pushes price up to grab those stops (liquidity), but the real intention is DOWN.
The failure to close above + lower low confirms the institutional move. This works in
reverse too (Asia low fakeout → bullish trap). Best on XAUUSD during London open
(08:00 UTC) or NY open (13:30 UTC).

06
Multi-Timeframe Check
(1m / 3m / 5m)
Before pulling the trigger, run a quick top-down check. The 5min tells
you the structure, the 3min tells you the shift, and the 1min gives
you the entry trigger. When all three align — A+++.
5MIN — STRUCTURE
This is your map. Look for:
 Overall trend direction
 Key FVG / BPR / OB zones
 Where price is in the bigger picture
3MIN — SHIFT (MSS / BOS)
This shows you the change. Look for:
 Market Structure Shift (MSS) — first higher
high after lows
 Break of Structure (BOS) confirmation
 Pullback into a smaller FVG
1MIN — TRIGGER
This is your entry. Look for:
 Engulfing / Hammer at the level
 Strong volume rejection candle
 Enter on the close of the trigger candle

 THE LAST PLAY
 Wait for the Market
to Show His Hand First
 ◆
 Every setup in this playbook works only when you wait.
 
Don't predict where the market will go. Let it show you first.
 
Wait for the FVG to form. Wait for the candle to CLOSE. Wait for the
 structure to shift. Wait for the trigger.
 
The market reveals its hand through price action — engulfing candles,
 liquidity sweeps, fakeouts, BOS. Your job is not to guess, but to
 recognize what's already happening.
■ THE 3 RULES OF PATIENCE
1. No setup? No trade. Sitting in cash is a position.
2. Candle not closed? Don't enter. Wicks lie. Closes don't.
3. Doubting it? Skip it. There's always a next setup.
 "The best trade is often the one you don't take."
